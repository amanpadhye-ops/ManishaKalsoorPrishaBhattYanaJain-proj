Global CAGR 17.1% India CAGR about 16.5%

The global event management software market was valued at USD 17.0 billion in 2025 and is forecast to reach USD 96.5 billion by 2036, a CAGR of 17.1% (Future Market Insights [FMI], 2026). India is one of the fastest-growing markets. Its event management software market was valued at about USD 372 million in 2025, and IMARC projects it will reach about USD 1.53 billion by 2034, growing at roughly 16.5% a year.

The software side covers ticketing, registration, venue management, planning and marketing. The services side covers managed and professional services. End users include corporates, government, education and third-party planners. The market is shifting to cloud delivery and growing across both SMEs and large enterprises.

Demand is fragmented, and many organisers still run events through spreadsheets, WhatsApp groups and separate vendors. A single platform serving all stakeholders therefore has room to grow, though it is also an unproven idea in India.

C2

Market trends

Several developments are shaping the event technology industry.

Hybrid and virtual events

Events are no longer only in person. IMARC projects a 70% rise in virtual events and a 65% rise in hybrid events, driven by post-pandemic habits and demand for flexible participation. Hybrid formats reach wider audiences at lower logistical cost and capture better data on audience behaviour. Vendors are adding live-streaming, virtual booths and digital networking lounges to recreate the in-person experience (IMARC Group, n.d.).

AI and personalisation

About 48% of major Indian industries had adopted AI by 2024, and nearly 75% of organisations were evaluating it. Event platforms are building AI in too. They use it to recommend sessions, send personalised notifications, read attendee sentiment, show live analytics and answer queries through chatbots (IMARC Group, n.d.).

One platform instead of many tools

Organisers want planning, registration, onsite management and analytics in one place. This suits IntelliConvene’s “operating system” idea.

C3

Competitors, direct and indirect

Direct competitors

Integrated event-management platforms competing for the same organisers.

Zoho Backstage

An event-management platform offering event websites, registration, ticketing, event promotion and attendee engagement. It competes directly through its integrated capabilities.

Cvent

Offers comprehensive event planning, registration, venue sourcing and event marketing solutions, particularly for corporate and large-scale events.

Airmeet

Specialises in virtual and hybrid events, webinars and networking experiences, competing for professional and corporate event audiences.

Eventbrite

Focuses on event discovery, ticketing, registration and event promotion, competing for organisers and attendees.

Indirect competitors

Alternatives that solve one part of the problem or avoid software altogether.

WhatsApp and Google Sheets

Frequently used for informal event communication, coordination, attendee tracking and vendor management.

Traditional event management agencies

Offer end-to-end event planning and coordination, an alternative to technology-led self-management.

Specialised vendor platforms

Venue-booking, catering and sponsorship networks compete for individual services within the event ecosystem.
